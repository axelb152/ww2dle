# WW**2**dle

A daily World War II battle guessing game, forked from
[Battldle](https://github.com/axelb152/battldle), itself inspired by
https://worldle.teuteuf.fr !

Guess the battle from the outline map of its surroundings: each guess
returns the distance and compass direction to the answer, with 6 tries and
a shareable emoji result. The battle's year is revealed as a hint after the
3rd wrong guess and its theater after the 5th.

### DISCLAIMER

Thank you for exploring this military-history guessing game. I want to
emphatically express that although this game is set against the backdrop of
World War II battles, I do not condone war and violence in any manner. The
intention behind creating this game is not to celebrate nor glorify conflict
but to offer an engaging game, intertwined with historical context, without
advocating for violence. I recognize World War II as one of the most painful
and distressing chapters of our shared history. The game deliberately
includes only battles and military operations — sites of atrocities are
excluded — and is intended to stimulate the same skills used in games like
Wordle, Worldle, etc., while respecting the real-world implications of
military conflict. As you navigate through your guesses, I hope you enjoy
the challenge whilst also keeping a mindful perspective on the larger themes
of history and peace.

## Map images

All battle map images are generated by `scripts/generate-images.ts` from
[Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/) public-domain geodata
(land polygons, coastlines, and admin-0 boundaries stored in
`scripts/data/`). No third-party imagery is used. To regenerate:

```
npx ts-node --compiler-options '{"module":"commonjs","downlevelIteration":true}' scripts/generate-images.ts
```

The dataset itself lives in `src/domain/battles.ts` (120 battles,
1939–1945, across 8 theaters) and can be validated with:

```
npx ts-node --compiler-options '{"module":"commonjs","downlevelIteration":true}' scripts/check-battles.ts
```
